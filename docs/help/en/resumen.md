# Concepto

The Summary is the first screen you see when you log in to Sunalyze. It concentrates the global state of your workspace: aggregated metrics, the five most recently modified projects, and shortcuts to start working without going through other views.

## Global indicators

At the top, four metric cards are calculated automatically across all your projects:

:::cards
- **Active projects** — projects that have moved past the «Draft» state.
- **Designed capacity** — total kWp across all projects, including drafts.
- **Reports** — projects in the «Report» state ready to sign.
- **Drafts** — projects still in progress.
:::

## Recent projects

The table shows up to five projects ordered by last modification. Click any row to open the design directly. The columns are **Client**, **kWp**, **Status** and **Modified**.

## Quick action panel

To the right of recent projects you have two buttons: **New design** starts a blank design, and **Duplicate a template** takes you to the project list filtered by templates.

# Tutorial

## Create your first project

:::steps
1. If the workspace is empty, the welcome screen shows the four steps of the flow: create, choose equipment, review the analysis, and generate the technical report.
2. Click **Create your first project** — the new design wizard opens.
3. Once you have projects, the **New design** button in the top bar serves the same purpose.
:::

## Navigate from the summary

:::steps
1. Click any row in «Recent projects» to open that design directly.
2. Click **See all** to go to the full project list with filters and search.
:::

:::tip{tone=info title="Solar resource for the area"}
The bottom right panel shows the annual irradiation and optimal tilt angle for the last location consulted. This is indicative; the real value is calculated in the analysis step using the project data.
:::
