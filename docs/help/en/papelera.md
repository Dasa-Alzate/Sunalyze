# Concepto

The bin holds the projects and catalogues you have deleted. Items stay here until you restore them; permanent deletion is not exposed in the interface.

:::cards
- **Proyectos** — deleted projects accessible to users with the **project:delete** permission.
- **Catálogos** — deleted catalogues accessible to users with the **catalog:manage** permission.
:::

# Tutorial

## Restore a project or catalogue

:::steps
1. Open **Papelera** in the side menu.
2. Find the item in the **Proyectos** or **Catálogos** section.
3. Click **Restaurar** on the right of the item.
4. Confirm in the dialog that appears; the item is returned to its original list immediately.
:::

:::tip{tone=success}
After restoring a project it reappears in your project list with all its data intact. Restored catalogues return to your equipment library.
:::

:::callout{tone=warning}
If the bin is empty or you cannot see certain items, you may not have the necessary permissions. Talk to your organisation administrator.
:::
